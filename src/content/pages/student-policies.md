## School Policies


Please review the important policies we have in place at Passport School, in the “School Policies” section of the page. 

#### **Attendance
**
The Attendance policy outlines the school board policies and school rules about absenteeism and tardiness for all students. [Click here for the District’s attendance policy.](https://www.ocps.net/135021_3)

 

#### **Code of Student Conduct
**
The Code of Student Conduct outlines the standards of behavior expected of students in grades PK-12 in all OCPS schools. It also provides details about appropriate student dress, school bus conduct, internet/technology policies, cell phone policies, and the code of civility. It is available in English, Spanish, Haitian-Creole, Portuguese, Vietnamese, and Arabic for your convenience here: **Click here for [Code of Student Conduct Document](https://www.ocps.net/code-of-student-conduct)**

 **Student-Parent Handbook**The Student-Parent Handbook has been written to provide important information concerning specific rules, policies, and procedures related to the safety and operation of our school. In order for Passport School to operate safely and efficiently, you and your child should become familiar with the expectations, procedures and rules outlined in this handbook.
