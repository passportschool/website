#### **Privacy Policy**

#### **1. Introduction**
Welcome to **Passport School Inc** ("Company," "we," "our," "us"). Your privacy is important to us, and we are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your information.

By using our website, services, or opting in to receive SMS communications, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

#### **2. Information We Collect**
We collect the following types of personal information:

- **Personal Identifiable Information (PII):** Name, email address, phone number, and other contact details.
- **SMS Consent Information:** Opt-in/opt-out status for SMS communications.
- **Usage Data:** Information about how you interact with our website and services.
- **Payment Information:** If applicable, we collect payment details for transactions.

#### **3. How We Use Your Information**
We use your personal information to:

- Provide and improve our services.
- Communicate with you regarding school-related updates, events, and important announcements.
- Respond to inquiries and customer service requests.
- Send SMS notifications for important updates, reminders, and alerts.

#### **4. How We Share Your Information**
We do **not** sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We may share your information in the following circumstances:

- **Service Providers:** We may share information with third-party vendors who assist in providing our services (e.g., SMS delivery providers, payment processors, IT support).
- **Legal Compliance:** If required by law or in response to legal processes, we may disclose personal information to regulatory authorities or law enforcement.

#### **SMS Consent Policy**
**We do not share SMS consent with third parties for marketing purposes.** Your SMS opt-in status is used exclusively for school-related notifications and will not be sold or shared with advertisers.

#### **5. Your Choices and Rights**

- **Opt-Out of SMS Communications:** You can unsubscribe from SMS messages at any time by replying **STOP** to any message you receive from us.
- **Access, Update, or Delete Your Information:** You may request to access, correct, or delete your personal data by contacting us at office@passportcharter.org.

#### **6. Data Security**
We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information. However, no system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute data security.

#### **7. External Links**
Our website may contain links to third-party sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these sites.

#### **8. Changes to This Privacy Policy**
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
