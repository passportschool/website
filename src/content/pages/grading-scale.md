#### **Grading Scale**
When appropriate, the following grading scale is used on individual assignments and as a guide when averaging report card grades. This is a decision made by the state and is expected to be followed by all schools.


#### **Final Grades**
The final grades will be calculated in the following manner:

**Semester 1 Grade** = 40%

**Semester 2 Grade** = 40%

**Final Exam Grade** = 20%

To access student’s grades from click on the link: [Skyward.ocps.net](https://parents.classlink.com/ocps)
