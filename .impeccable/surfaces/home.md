# Surface brief — homepage (primary target: passportcharter.org)

Scope: homepage; establishes the system all 23 inner pages inherit. Visitor mode: Persuade for prospective families + Operate for current parents.

Audience: current parents (phones, quick answers), prospective families (enrollment), students/staff (resources). Job: reach news, events, enrollment, or the parent hub in one scroll. Constraints: preserve brand (maroon #590304, Barlow, real photos), WCAG 2.1 AA, conventional register, existing URL paths.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The canon at full fidelity — a modern district-site homepage parents trust on first sight; it refuses thematic art direction and template noise alike, earning credibility through type scale, spacing discipline, and real photography.
OWN-WORLD: Warm-white ground, ink text, brand maroon carrying the header, buttons, links, and section anchors; Barlow throughout with strong weight steps; standard components (utility bar, grouped sticky nav, real-photo hero, card grids, fat footer) at high polish; one system across all 24 pages.
STORY: A visitor lands on a real photograph of the school, reads the mission in plain sight, and reaches news, events, enrollment information, or the parent hub within one scroll and zero thought.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width real photo of the school, maroon headline with the mission sentence, two actions — "Enrollment Information" (primary button) and "For Parents" (secondary); upcoming events and latest news cards immediately below. Nav: logo left, Parents / Students / School groups, Enrollment CTA right.
FORM: The standing exit (canon), craft bar = modern district sites, Apptegy/Edlio-class register, named by the owner 2026-11-14. seed: none (unavailable)
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
